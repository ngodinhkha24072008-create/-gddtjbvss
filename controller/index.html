Using it on your phone

  1. Pair the HC-05 / HC-06 in Android Bluetooth settings first (PIN is usually 1234).
  2. Tap Connect robot, allow Nearby devices the first time, and pick the module.
  3. Open this controller in Chrome on Android from its web address (it needs https). Bluetooth can't be used inside the claude.ai preview.
  4. Turn the phone sideways and tap Full screen to hide the browser bars.
  5. HM-10 / BLE module: tap Connect BLE and pick the module.
  6. HC-05 / HC-06: pair it in Android Bluetooth settings first (PIN is usually 1234), then tap Connect HC-05. Needs a recent Chrome.
  7. The Arduino side is CarArm4DOF.ino at 9600 baud. Arm buttons move a joint 5° per step and repeat while held.
