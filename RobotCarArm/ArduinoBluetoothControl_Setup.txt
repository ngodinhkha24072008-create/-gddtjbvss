ROBOT CAR + ARM : CONTROL SCHEME FOR "ARDUINO BLUETOOTH CONTROL" (broxcode)
==========================================================================

The phone app does not run code. It only sends the characters you give each
control. The program that runs on the robot is RobotCarArm.ino. Upload that to
the Arduino first, then type the values below into the app's settings.

Bluetooth module : HC-05 / HC-06 on D0 (RX) and D1 (TX), 9600 baud
Pair it in Android Bluetooth settings first (PIN 1234 or 0000).


1) GAMEPAD / ARROW KEYS SCREEN  (drive the car + arm buttons)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Open the screen, tap the settings (gear) icon and set each control to send:

  Control              Send    What the robot does
  -------------------  ------  ------------------------------------------
  Up                   F       Drive forward
  Down                 B       Drive backward
  Left                 L       Turn left
  Right                R       Turn right
  Up + Left            G       Forward-left   (only if the app offers diagonals)
  Up + Right           I       Forward-right  (only if the app offers diagonals)
  Down + Left          H       Back-left      (only if the app offers diagonals)
  Down + Right         J       Back-right     (only if the app offers diagonals)
  On release (if any)  S       Stop driving   (optional, the car also stops by
                                               itself 0.4 s after you let go)

  Triangle  (△)        W       Save arm position (each tap fills the next of
                               5 slots, LED 1-5 lights up)
  Circle    (○)        U       Play the saved positions on a loop
  Cross     (✕)        u       Stop playback (lower-case u)
  Square    (□)        X       Clear all saved positions (LEDs turn off)
  Start or Select      D       STOP EVERYTHING (car + arm playback)

  Leave "continuous sending while held" ON for the direction buttons.
  Letters are case-sensitive: U and u do different things.


2) BUTTONS & SLIDER SCREEN  (hand open / close lever)
-----------------------------------------------------
Turn the phone sideways to show the slider, open its settings and set:

  Slider minimum       0       Hand fully CLOSED  (slider all the way left)
  Slider maximum       10      Hand fully OPEN    (slider all the way right)

  The six buttons on this screen can repeat the arm controls if you like:
  Button 1 = W (save)   Button 2 = U (play)   Button 3 = u (stop playback)
  Button 4 = X (clear)  Button 5 = D (stop all)  Button 6 = S (stop car)

  Using a different slider range (for example 0-100)? Change LEVER_MIN and
  LEVER_MAX near the top of RobotCarArm.ino to the same numbers.


3) QUICK TEST WITH THE TERMINAL SCREEN
--------------------------------------
Type these one at a time and press send. Wheels off the ground for the first ones.

  F        wheels turn forward for about 0.4 s, then stop (auto-stop works)
  S        nothing moving
  W        "Saved 1" comes back, LED 1 lights
  0        hand closes
  10       hand opens fully
  U        arm moves through saved positions
  u        arm goes back to following the knobs
  D        everything stops


4) THINGS TO ADJUST IN RobotCarArm.ino IF NEEDED
------------------------------------------------
  GRIPPER               which servo is the hand (0, 1 or 2; default 2 = D9)
  GRIPPER_CLOSED_ANGLE  servo angle when closed (default 10)
  GRIPPER_OPEN_ANGLE    servo angle when open   (default 170)
                        swap these two if the slider works backwards
  DRIVE_TIMEOUT_MS      how long the car keeps going after the last command
                        (default 400 ms)
  Turns the wrong way?  swap the L/R letters in the app settings.
